This Experience Only Strategy Agreement (“Agreement”) is entered into between LIMOVI Gold Ecosystem Private Limited (“LIMOVI”) and the Customer accepting this Agreement (“Customer”), and forms an integral part of the Master Customer & Platform Agreement (“MCA”) accepted by the Customer.

1. DEFINITIONS AND APPLICATION

1.1 Capitalised terms not defined in this Agreement shall have the meanings assigned to them under the MCA.

1.2 For the purposes of this Agreement:

(a) “Experience” means the temporary use of Jewellery by the Customer in accordance with this Agreement.

(b) “Experience Period” means the period specified at the time of booking during which the Customer is authorised to possess and use the selected Jewellery.

(c) “Forfeiture” means the appropriation by LIMOVI, in whole or in part, of the Security provided by the Customer, upon the occurrence of a forfeiture event expressly specified in this Agreement, in accordance with the terms of this Agreement and applicable law.

(d) “Forfeiture Event” means the occurrence of a material default by the Customer in relation to the return of the Jewellery, where the Customer fails to return the Jewellery upon expiry of the Experience Period or any approved extension and continues to fail to return the Jewellery despite receiving a written or electronic notice from LIMOVI requiring the Customer to return the Jewellery within twenty-four (24) hours from delivery or deemed delivery of such notice in accordance with the applicable notice provisions of the MCA.

(e) “Jewellery” means the ornament or ornaments selected by the Customer and made available by LIMOVI for the Experience.

(f) “Security” means the money, physical gold, Digital Gold or other permitted asset furnished or acquired by or on behalf of the Customer in accordance with this Agreement to secure the Customer’s obligations relating to the Experience.

(g) “Security Return Basis” means the agreed basis on which the Security is to be returned or settled upon completion or termination of the Experience.

1.3 This Agreement governs the rights and obligations of the parties specific to the Experience Only Strategy and shall be read with the MCA and applicable Jewellery Experience Service Terms. The MCA shall govern matters of general application, including platform use, privacy, indemnity, limitation of liability, notices and dispute resolution, except where this Agreement expressly governs a Strategy-specific matter.

2. SELECTION OF JEWELLERY AND SECURITY

2.1 Displayed Value and Security Requirement. Before a transaction is confirmed, LIMOVI shall disclose to the Customer the displayed value of the selected Jewellery (the “Displayed Value”). The Displayed Value shall be determined by reference to the valuation basis, applicable rate, currency and calculation method specified in the applicable Transaction Confirmation. The amount or value of Security required for the selected Jewellery shall be determined by LIMOVI and communicated to the Customer in the applicable Transaction Confirmation before the Customer accepts the transaction. The Customer shall provide or constitute such Security in accordance with Clause

2.2 and the applicable Transaction Confirmation, and the provision or constitution of the required Security shall be a condition precedent to the release of the Jewellery to the Customer.

2.2 Security may be furnished in money, physical gold or Digital Gold, or through the acquisition of physical gold or Digital Gold using money, where the relevant arrangement is accepted by LIMOVI. The applicable form, amount or quantity of Security and the Security Return Basis shall be agreed before the Experience commences.

2.3 Where Security is furnished in gold, the quantity and purity shall be recorded. Where money is applied towards the acquisition of gold or Digital Gold, the transaction terms shall specify whether the Customer’s entitlement is to return of the monetary amount or to return or settlement by reference to the quantity of gold actually acquired.

2.4 The displayed value of the Jewellery shall determine the Security required. No separate valuation of the Jewellery by the Customer shall be required for this purpose.

2.5 The Customer shall furnish the required Security and pay the applicable Experience Charges before taking possession of the Jewellery. LIMOVI shall be entitled to withhold release until these conditions are satisfied.

2.6 Third-Party Providers and Custodians. Where any Security is received, held, acquired, transferred, returned or settled through a third-party provider or custodian, the applicable Transaction Confirmation shall identify the provider or custodian and disclose the material terms and procedures relevant to the Customer's Security, including any material limitation on LIMOVI's control over the Security or its settlement. Third-party terms shall bind the Customer only to the extent that they are validly incorporated into the transaction or otherwise accepted by the Customer as required by applicable law. Nothing in this clause authorises LIMOVI to promise a return, transfer or settlement that it is not contractually or practically able to procure.

3. EXPERIENCE PERIOD AND POSSESSION

3.1 Transaction Confirmation and Acceptance Before the Jewellery is released, LIMOVI shall make available to the Customer a Transaction Confirmation identifying the selected Jewellery, its displayed value, the applicable Security, the Security Return Basis, the Experience Period, the return deadline, the applicable charges and such other material transaction-specific terms as may be applicable. The Customer shall be given access to this Agreement and the applicable Transaction Confirmation before confirming the transaction. The Customer shall electronically accept this Agreement and the Transaction Confirmation through the designated acceptance mechanism. The Jewellery shall not be released until the required acceptance has been recorded, the applicable Security has been received or validly constituted in accordance with the agreed arrangement, and the applicable charges have been paid.

3.2 Permitted Use and Experience Period. The Customer may possess and use the Jewellery solely for the purpose and during the Experience Period specified in the applicable Transaction Confirmation. The Customer shall return the Jewellery in accordance with Clause 4 and shall not deal with it except as expressly permitted under this Agreement. The Customer's possession is temporary and does not confer ownership or any other proprietary interest in the Jewellery.

3.3 The Customer acknowledges that the Jewellery may belong to a third-party owner and that the Experience does not confer ownership or any proprietary interest in the Jewellery. The Customer shall hold and use the Jewellery subject to the rights of its owner and the terms of this Agreement.

3.4 Condition and Identification at Delivery. Before releasing the Jewellery, LIMOVI shall record its identification and condition by means of such records as may be reasonably appropriate, including photographs, video, a condition report, identifying marks, details of stones and components, and weight or purity particulars where relevant. The Customer shall be given a reasonable opportunity to inspect the Jewellery at delivery and shall promptly notify LIMOVI of any apparent defect, pre-existing damage, missing component or discrepancy. The condition records maintained at delivery may be relied upon in determining any subsequent claim for damage, breakage, loss or missing components, subject to the evidence available and applicable law.

3.5 Care, Custody and Incident Reporting. From the time the Jewellery is handed over to the Customer until its return is acknowledged in accordance with Clause 4.3, the Customer shall exercise reasonable care, keep the Jewellery secure, comply with the applicable handling, storage and return instructions, and use it only for the permitted purpose. The Customer shall promptly notify LIMOVI of any loss, theft, damage, breakage, suspected unauthorised access, seizure or third-party claim affecting the Jewellery, and shall reasonably cooperate in preserving evidence, making reports required by law and facilitating any lawful recovery or insurance claim. The Customer shall not knowingly conceal any such incident or materially relevant information concerning it.

3.6 Handover Acknowledgement. The handover of the Jewellery may be evidenced by an electronic acknowledgement, delivery confirmation, authenticated receipt or other reliable record identifying the Customer, the Jewellery and the relevant transaction. The Customer shall verify the identity and particulars of the Jewellery at handover and acknowledge receipt through the designated procedure.

3.7 Unauthorised Dealings and No Right of Retention. The Customer shall not sell, pledge, mortgage, encumber, lend, transfer, deliver to another person, substitute, alter, dismantle, repair or otherwise dispose of the Jewellery, or permit any unauthorised person to possess or use it. The Customer shall not retain the Jewellery as security for, or in purported satisfaction of, any refund, claim, dispute or other amount asserted against LIMOVI or any third-party owner, except to the extent that such retention is expressly permitted by non-waivable applicable law. Any request for a refund, adjustment or other relief shall be pursued separately and shall not, by itself, extend the Experience Period or the return deadline.

4. RETURN OF JEWELLERY

4.1 The Customer shall return the Jewellery to LIMOVI or its designated recipient, at the designated place and by the date and time specified for the transaction.

4.2 The Customer shall return the Jewellery together with all components, stones, accessories, tags, cases or other items delivered with it, in the condition in which it was received, subject to any pre-existing condition expressly recorded and ordinary wear expressly permitted under the applicable Service Terms.

4.3 Responsibility Until Acknowledged Return. The Customer shall remain responsible for compliance with the obligations relating to the Jewellery until the Jewellery and all items required to accompany it have been returned to LIMOVI or its authorised recipient in accordance with the designated return procedure and the return has been acknowledged by an electronic confirmation, receipt or other reliable record. The Customer shall retain reasonable evidence of dispatch or tender where return is arranged through an authorised delivery service. Dispatch alone shall not constitute completed return unless the applicable transaction terms expressly provide otherwise.

4.4 LIMOVI may examine the returned Jewellery and compare its condition with the condition at delivery. The Customer shall be given reasonable particulars of any alleged damage, breakage, missing component or other discrepancy and, where appropriate, an opportunity to respond before a deduction is finalised.

4.5 The Experience Period may be extended only with LIMOVI’s prior written or electronic consent. A request for extension shall not extend the return deadline unless the extension is accepted. Any additional charges and revised return deadline shall be disclosed to the Customer.

5. DAMAGE, BREAKAGE AND LOSS

5.1 Basis and Extent of Liability. The Customer shall be liable for loss of, damage to, breakage of or deterioration in the Jewellery to the extent that such loss, damage, breakage or deterioration is attributable to an act or omission of the Customer, negligence, misuse, failure to exercise the care required under this Agreement, breach of this Agreement, or an act or omission of a person whom the Customer permitted to access, possess or use the Jewellery. Liability shall be assessed in accordance with this Agreement and applicable law, having regard to the condition of the Jewellery at handover, the circumstances of the incident, any applicable exclusions under Clause 5.6, and any insurance recovery or other compensation received in respect of the same loss. The mere occurrence of loss, theft or damage while the Jewellery is in the Customer's possession shall not, by itself, establish liability irrespective of the circumstances.

5.2 Minor damage includes scratches, dents, deformation etc on the surface, loose settings, broken clasps, damaged chains, detached components and other damage that does not render the Jewellery wholly unusable but requires inspection, repair, restoration or replacement of a component. The Customer shall be liable for the reasonable and substantiated cost of rectifying such damage to the extent attributable to the Customer.

5.3 Major damage or breakage includes fracture or breakage of the ornament, loss or detachment of stones or material components, damage affecting structural integrity, material alteration of the Jewellery, or damage that materially diminishes its utility or value. The Customer shall be liable for the reasonable and substantiated repair or restoration cost or, where repair is not reasonably feasible, the loss properly established under applicable law and this Agreement.

5.4 Where any stone, component, accessory or part of the Jewellery is lost, removed, substituted or not returned, the Customer shall be liable for the reasonable cost of replacement or the loss properly established, subject to the evidence available and applicable law.

5.5 Loss, Theft, Disappearance or Non-Return. If the Jewellery is lost, stolen, cannot be located, or is not returned when required, the Customer shall notify LIMOVI promptly after becoming aware of the circumstances, provide information reasonably available to the Customer, preserve relevant evidence, and reasonably cooperate with lawful reporting, investigation, insurance and recovery procedures. The Customer's financial liability, if any, shall be determined under Clauses 5.1, 5.6 and 5.7 and Clause 6, as applicable, based on the circumstances, the extent to which the loss or non-return is attributable to the Customer, and the loss reasonably established. Nothing in this clause creates automatic liability merely because the Jewellery was stolen or disappeared while in the Customer's possession, or limits liability that otherwise arises under applicable law.

5.6 Matters Excluded from Customer Liability. Subject to applicable law, the Customer shall not be liable to the extent that loss of, damage to, breakage of or deterioration in the Jewellery results from: (a) a pre-existing condition recorded at handover; (b) ordinary wear expressly permitted by this Agreement; (c) an inherent defect not caused or aggravated by the Customer; or (d) an event or circumstance not attributable to the Customer under the applicable contractual and legal standards. This clause does not exclude liability for any loss or damage that the Customer caused, aggravated or failed reasonably to mitigate where such liability arises under this Agreement or applicable law.

5.7 LIMOVI shall determine any proposed damage or loss claim on the basis of reasonable evidence, which may include delivery and return photographs, condition records, repair estimates, invoices, expert assessment or other relevant material. Any amount claimed shall be proportionate to the loss properly established, taking account of the condition of the Jewellery before the Experience and any applicable insurance recovery.

5.8 The Customer shall not independently repair, alter or dispose of damaged Jewellery without LIMOVI’s prior consent, except where immediate action is reasonably necessary to prevent further damage or comply with law. The Customer shall preserve the Jewellery and relevant evidence pending instructions.

6. LATE RETURN, NON RETURN AND CONTRACTUAL CHARGES

6.1 If the Customer fails to return the Jewellery by the agreed deadline, the Customer shall promptly return it and shall be liable for any late-return charge expressly disclosed and accepted at the time of booking.

6.2 Any late-return charge shall be calculated in accordance with the rate, duration and method specified in the applicable transaction terms. It shall represent a reasonable and proportionate amount agreed in advance in respect of the anticipated consequences of delayed return and shall not operate as an arbitrary or punitive forfeiture.

6.3 Payment of a late-return charge shall not entitle the Customer to retain the Jewellery beyond the agreed Experience Period or any extension expressly approved by LIMOVI.

6.4 Where delayed return causes additional proven loss beyond the applicable late-return charge, LIMOVI may seek such further compensation as is recoverable under the MCA, this Agreement and applicable law, subject to the prohibition against double recovery.

6.5 Failure to Return and Remedies. Failure to return the Jewellery by the applicable deadline, unless the deadline has been validly extended in writing or the failure is excused under this Agreement or applicable law, constitutes a breach of this Agreement. LIMOVI may give the Customer written or electronic notice requiring return within a reasonable period having regard to the circumstances, without prejudice to the right to require earlier return where reasonably necessary to protect the Jewellery or its owner. If the Customer fails to return the Jewellery, LIMOVI may pursue lawful recovery and claim charges and losses recoverable under this Agreement and applicable law. Any amount claimed shall be reasonably substantiated, proportionate to the loss or contractual charge concerned, and subject to the prohibition on double recovery. Nothing in this clause authorises unlawful force, seizure or any unsupported allegation of criminal conduct.

6.6 The Customer shall not be charged a late-return penalty where the delay is attributable solely to LIMOVI’s failure to accept a timely tender of return or another circumstance for which the Customer is not contractually responsible.

6.7 Unauthorised Retention After Expiry. Upon expiry of the Experience Period, the Customer shall cease using the Jewellery and return it in accordance with Clause 4, unless an extension has been agreed in writing or electronically by LIMOVI, specifying the revised deadline and any applicable additional charges. If the Customer continues to retain or use the Jewellery without such an extension, LIMOVI may demand its return and pursue the remedies available under Clause 6.5 and applicable law. Subject to applicable law and proof of the relevant loss, LIMOVI may claim any applicable late-return charges and reasonable, substantiated losses caused by the unauthorised retention, including documented recovery costs where recoverable and demonstrated loss arising from the Jewellery's unavailability to its owner or for a confirmed subsequent booking. Any such claim shall take account of mitigation, insurance or other recoveries, and amounts already recovered, and shall not permit double recovery. The Displayed Value or amount of Security shall not, merely by being specified, constitute an automatic penalty or conclusive measure of compensation. Acceptance of a late return, a part-payment, or a request for an extension shall not by itself waive an existing breach or accrued right, unless LIMOVI expressly agrees otherwise in writing or electronically. This clause does not permit recovery by unlawful means or exclude any defence available to the Customer under applicable law.

6.8 Forfeiture of Security upon Failure to Return Jewellery: If the Customer fails to return the Jewellery upon expiry of the Experience Period or any approved extension, LIMOVI shall issue a written or electronic notice requiring the Customer to return the Jewellery within twenty-four (24) hours from delivery or deemed delivery of such notice in accordance with the applicable notice provisions of the MCA. If the Customer fails to return the Jewellery within the aforesaid period, a Forfeiture Event shall occur, and LIMOVI shall be entitled to invoke Forfeiture of the Security, in whole or in part, in accordance with this Agreement and applicable law. Forfeiture shall be without prejudice to LIMOVI’s right to pursue lawful recovery of the Jewellery and including the costs or loss incurred by LIMOVI due to the default of the Customer and any other remedies available under this Agreement.

7. SECURITY RETURN AND SETTLEMENT

7.1 Upon expiry or earlier completion of the Experience, and subject to return of the Jewellery and settlement of any amount properly recoverable under this Agreement, LIMOVI shall arrange return or settlement of the Security in accordance with the Security Return Basis accepted by the Customer.

7.2 Agreed Security Return Basis. Subject to the deductions permitted under Clause 7.5, the Security shall be returned or settled according to the Security Return Basis expressly selected and recorded in the applicable Transaction Confirmation, as follows:

(a) Money: the agreed monetary amount shall be returned.

(b) Physical Gold: the agreed quantity and purity of gold shall be returned or otherwise settled as expressly agreed.

(c) Digital Gold: the recorded quantity of Digital Gold, or the agreed monetary entitlement, shall be returned or settled according to the selected basis and the applicable provider's disclosed and accepted terms.

(d) Money Used to Acquire Gold or Digital Gold: the Transaction Confirmation shall expressly state whether the Customer's entitlement is to the original monetary amount, a specified quantity of gold or Digital Gold, or a monetary amount calculated by reference to an agreed gold-value formula. The acquisition of gold or Digital Gold using the Customer's money shall not, by itself, determine which entitlement applies.

(e) Gold-Value Settlement: where the parties agree to a monetary settlement linked to gold value, the Transaction Confirmation shall specify the applicable gold quantity or purity, rate source, reference date or event, calculation method, and any expressly agreed transaction costs or adjustments.

The applicable Transaction Confirmation shall record the selected basis with sufficient clarity to identify the Customer's entitlement and the method by which it is calculated. No alternative basis shall apply unless agreed in writing or electronically by the parties.

7.3 Where the agreed entitlement is return of a fixed monetary amount, a subsequent fluctuation in the market price of gold shall not alter that amount. Where the agreed entitlement is return of a specified quantity and purity of gold, the Customer’s entitlement shall be determined by that quantity and purity and not by its subsequent monetary value.

7.4 Where the parties agree that the entitlement is calculated by reference to a gold rate, the applicable rate source, purity, reference date and calculation method shall be specified before the Experience commences. Unless expressly agreed otherwise, the relevant reference date shall be the date on which the monetary deposit is received, the physical gold is accepted, or the acquisition of gold or Digital Gold is completed, as applicable.

7.5 Application of Security and Deductions. LIMOVI may apply or deduct from the Security only amounts that are due and recoverable under this Agreement, the applicable Transaction Confirmation and applicable law, including properly payable charges and substantiated amounts recoverable for damage, loss, missing components, late return or other breach, as applicable. Any deduction shall be reasonably calculated and supported by an explanation of its basis and the relevant available records. Before making a disputed deduction, LIMOVI shall provide the Customer with written or electronic notice identifying the proposed deduction, its calculation and the supporting particulars reasonably available, and shall allow the Customer a reasonable opportunity to respond. This requirement shall not prevent urgent action reasonably necessary to preserve the Jewellery or protect a person's legal rights. Where Security is held by, or is to be returned or settled through, a third-party provider or custodian, any application, deduction or settlement shall be subject to the rights and procedures actually available under the applicable arrangement and disclosed terms. LIMOVI shall not represent that it can unilaterally appropriate or direct the disposition of Security where it lacks the contractual authority or practical ability to do so. A dispute concerning one part of a claim shall not, by itself, allow LIMOVI to withhold an otherwise due and undisputed balance of the Security. Settlement of the undisputed balance shall be dealt with under Clause 7.7. Any amount claimed in excess of the available Security may be pursued separately only to the extent recoverable under this Agreement and applicable law.

7.6 Any undisputed balance of the Security shall be returned or settled within the agreed settlement period, notwithstanding a dispute concerning a separate portion, to the extent reasonably practicable and legally permissible.

7.7 Settlement Timeline and Records. LIMOVI shall process the return or settlement of the Security within the period specified in the applicable Transaction Confirmation, subject to completion of the required return and inspection procedures and any lawful, properly supported deductions under this Agreement. Where a third-party provider or custodian is responsible for holding or processing the Security, LIMOVI shall disclose any material settlement dependency or procedure applicable to the transaction and shall take the steps reasonably required of it under the relevant arrangement. LIMOVI shall maintain or procure reasonable transaction records identifying the Security received or constituted, the agreed Security Return Basis, the calculation of any settlement, each deduction made, and the amount or quantity returned or remaining payable. These records shall be made available to the Customer to the extent required by this Agreement or applicable law, subject to lawful privacy and confidentiality restrictions.

7.8 No interest, appreciation, investment return or profit shall be payable merely because the Security is furnished under this Strategy, unless expressly agreed in writing and legally permissible.

10.4 Upon cancellation or termination, the Customer shall return any Jewellery already delivered. LIMOVI shall return or settle the Security after accounting for amounts properly recoverable under this Agreement.

10.5 Termination shall not affect accrued rights and liabilities or any provision intended by its nature to survive termination.
