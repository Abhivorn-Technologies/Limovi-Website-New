This Enrol & Experience Strategy Agreement (“Strategy Agreement”) is entered into pursuant to and forms an integral part of the LIMOVI Master Customer & Platform Agreement (“MCA”) and governs the enrolment, custody, listing and Experience of Jewellery owned by the Customer.

PARTIES

LIMOVI GOLD ECOSYSTEM PRIVATE LIMITED, a company incorporated under the Companies Act, 2013, having its registered office at [________] (“LIMOVI”);

AND

The customer identified in the applicable onboarding record, Jewellery Enrolment Record, Account or Transaction Record (“Customer”).

LIMOVI and the Customer are collectively referred to as the “Parties”.

1. STATUS AND INCORPORATION UNDER THE MCA

1.1 This Strategy Agreement is a Strategy-specific agreement entered into under the MCA and forms an integral part of the contractual relationship between LIMOVI and the Customer.

1.2 The Customer’s participation in this Strategy shall additionally be governed by: (a) the MCA; (b) this Strategy Agreement; (c) Schedule 1; (d) the Jewellery Enrolment Record / Onboarding Certificate; (e) the Common Jewellery Experience Terms; (f) applicable Insurance Documentation; (g) applicable Gold Liquidity / Sale Agreement; and (h) applicable lending partner documentation.

1.3 Capitalised terms not defined herein shall have the meanings assigned to them in the MCA.

1.4 The MCA shall govern all general matters, while this Strategy Agreement shall govern matters specifically relating to Customer-owned Jewellery enrolled under this Strategy.

1.5 In the event of inconsistency, the transaction-specific particulars shall prevail only for the relevant Transaction, Schedule 1 shall prevail for the commercial terms expressly specified therein, this Strategy Agreement shall prevail for Strategy-specific matters, and the MCA shall prevail for general matters.

2. NATURE OF THE STRATEGY

2.1 Under the Enrol & Experience Strategy, the Customer may deliver Jewellery lawfully owned by the Customer to LIMOVI for: (a) verification; (b) identification; (c) enrolment; (d) custody; (e) listing through the Jewellery Cloud; and (f) temporary Experience by eligible Customers.

2.2 Enrolment shall not constitute a sale or transfer of ownership of the Jewellery to LIMOVI.

2.3 The Customer shall remain the owner of the Enrolled Jewellery except to the extent ownership is subsequently transferred pursuant to a valid sale, transfer or other transaction expressly authorised under the applicable Service Terms.

3. ELIGIBILITY AND ACCEPTANCE

3.1 The Customer shall be eligible upon completion of: (a) KYC; (b) ownership verification; (c) purity verification; (d) weight verification; (e) authenticity verification; (f) applicable documentation; and (g) such other onboarding requirements as LIMOVI may prescribe.

3.2 Only Jewellery accepted by LIMOVI and assigned a Jewellery Identification Number (“JIN”) shall constitute “Enrolled Jewellery” and recorded in the Jewellery Enrolment Record forming part of this Agreement.

3.3 The Enrolment Record shall contain, to the extent applicable, the description of the Jewellery, photographs, gross weight, net gold weight, purity, hallmark particulars, stone particulars, invoice or ownership document details, condition and other identifying characteristics.

3.4 LIMOVI shall have the right to reject Jewellery where title, authenticity, purity, condition, ownership, documentation, insurance eligibility or legal compliance is not satisfactorily established.

4. TITLE AND DELIVERY INTO CUSTODY

4.1 The Customer shall remain the sole owner of Enrolled Jewellery.

4.2 Delivery of Jewellery to LIMOVI, a Vault Provider, Custodian, logistics provider or other authorised service provider shall not constitute: (a) sale; (b) transfer of ownership; (c) assignment; (d) pledge; or (e) creation of any proprietary interest, except where expressly provided by a separate written transaction.

4.3 The Customer represents and warrants that: (a) the Customer has lawful title to the Jewellery; (b) the Jewellery is not subject to any undisclosed lien, pledge, charge or encumbrance; (c) no third party has any undisclosed ownership or beneficial interest; (d) the Jewellery is not subject to any pending ownership dispute; and (e) the Customer has full authority to deliver the Jewellery for enrolment and custody.

4.4 Upon acceptance, LIMOVI shall hold possession of the Jewellery in the capacity applicable to the custody arrangement, including as custodian or bailee where the arrangement constitutes bailment under Applicable Law.

5. VERIFICATION AND JEWELLERY RECORD

5.1 LIMOVI may inspect, photograph, weigh, assay, test, verify hallmark and purity, assess condition and undertake such other verification as it considers reasonably necessary. LIMOVI may refuse any Jewellery which, in its reasonable assessment, does not satisfy the applicable eligibility criteria or presents concerns relating to authenticity, ownership, purity, condition, documentation, valuation or lawful origin.

5.2 The particulars established upon enrolment shall be recorded in the Jewellery Enrolment Record and/or Onboarding Certificate. The Onboarding Certificate shall serve as the principal identification record for the Enrolled Jewellery and shall be required for such exit, release or ownership-related procedures as may be prescribed by LIMOVI.

5.3 Issuance of the Onboarding Certificate shall not create or evidence any transfer of title in favour of LIMOVI.

5.4 The Jewellery Record shall, where applicable, contain: (a) JIN; (b) description; (c) photographs; (d) weight; (e) purity; (f) hallmark particulars; (g) design/category; (h) condition; (i) declared or assessed value; and (j) other identifying particulars.

5.5 The recorded particulars shall constitute the reference record for Experience and return verification, subject to manifest error or established discrepancy.

5.6 The Customer acknowledges that the Jewellery Record is intended to establish the condition and identity of the Jewellery at the commencement of custody and is not a guarantee of any future market value

6. JEWELLERY CLOUD

6.1 Upon enrolment, the Customer authorises LIMOVI to list the Enrolled Jewellery in the Jewellery Cloud.

6.2 LIMOVI may display such information as is reasonably necessary for operation of the Jewellery Cloud, including: (a) photographs; (b) description; (c) category; (d) weight; (e) purity; (f) Experience value; and (g) availability.

6.3 LIMOVI shall not be required to disclose information concerning the identity of the Customer to an Experience Customer except where required by Applicable Law or expressly authorised by the Customer.

6.4 During the period in which Jewellery is enrolled and available for Experience, the Owner shall not demand physical delivery of the Jewellery merely to inspect or temporarily use it unless LIMOVI permits such access in accordance with the applicable Service and operational requirements.The Customer shall not be entitled to approve or reject individual Experience Customers unless LIMOVI expressly provides such facility.

7. EXPERIENCE OF ENROLLED JEWELLERY

7.1 The Owner acknowledges that the purpose of enrolment is not merely storage but participation in LIMOVI's Jewellery Experience ecosystem, whereby Enrolled Jewellery may be made available to eligible Customers for temporary possession and use in accordance with the Common Jewellery Experience Terms.

7.2 The Customer’s ownership rights shall remain distinct from the temporary possessory or usage rights granted to an Experience Customer.

7.3 The Customer shall not interfere with an Experience validly initiated in accordance with the applicable Service Terms.

7.4 Experience shall remain subject to availability, eligibility, applicable charges, scheduling, logistics and the Common Jewellery Experience Terms.

7.5 The Owner acknowledges that Jewellery used for Experience may undergo reasonable ordinary wear and tear. Liability for damage beyond permitted ordinary wear and tear shall be determined in accordance with the applicable Jewellery Condition and Damage Standards.

7.6 The Owner shall not be required to bear Damage attributable to an Experience Customer merely because the Owner is the legal owner of the Jewellery.

8. OWNER BENEFIT

8.1 For each qualifying Experience completed using the Customer’s Enrolled Jewellery, the Customer shall be entitled to the Owner Benefit specified in Schedule 1.

8.2 No Owner Benefit shall accrue merely by reason of enrolment.

8.3 The Owner Benefit shall arise only upon completion of a qualifying Experience and shall be subject to: (a) applicable charges; (b) taxes; (c) withholding; (d) deductions; (e) reconciliation; and (f) settlement procedures.

8.4 LIMOVI shall make available through the Platform or applicable statement the Experience history and corresponding Owner Benefit records attributable to the Enrolled Jewellery.

9. CUSTOMER'S OWN EXPERIENCE

9.1 Subject to the Strategy and applicable eligibility requirements, the Customer may itself avail Experience against the Gold Balance or entitlement generated through enrolment.

9.2 The Customer’s use of its own Enrolled Jewellery shall not extinguish or transfer its ownership.

9.3 The Customer shall comply with the Common Jewellery Experience Terms while using the Jewellery.

10. CUSTODY AND MOVEMENT OF JEWELLERY

10.1 LIMOVI may place Enrolled Jewellery with a Vault Provider, Custodian, security partner, logistics provider or other authorised service provider.

10.2 LIMOVI may move Jewellery between authorised locations for: (a) custody; (b) Experience; (c) insurance; (d) inspection; (e) verification; (f) logistics; or (g) other authorised operational purposes.

10.3 The Customer acknowledges that physical possession may accordingly remain with an authorised custodian or service provider and not necessarily with LIMOVI at all times.

11. INSURANCE

11.1 Enrolled Jewellery shall be subject to such insurance arrangement as may be applicable to the relevant custody or Experience arrangement.

11.2 The insurer shall determine the terms and conditions of the applicable insurance policy, including: (a) insured value; (b) coverage; (c) exclusions; (d) deductibles; (e) conditions precedent; (f) claims procedure; and (g) settlement.

11.3 The Customer shall not independently procure or maintain an external insurance policy in respect of the same Jewellery where LIMOVI’s applicable arrangement requires the Jewellery to remain covered under the designated insurance arrangement.

11.4 Insurance shall not be construed as transferring ownership or altering the Customer’s title to the Jewellery.

11.5 Claims shall be administered in accordance with the applicable insurance policy and the applicable Service Terms.

11.6 Where Jewellery is lost or damaged while in the possession or responsibility of an Experience Customer, LIMOVI shall administer the matter in accordance with the applicable Experience Terms, damage-assessment mechanism and insurance arrangements.

11.7 The Owner shall not independently negotiate or settle a claim concerning Enrolled Jewellery with an Experience Customer unless expressly authorised by LIMOVI. In the event the Owner independently settles a claim without explicit authorization or knowledge of LIMOVI, LIMOVI shall not be responsible for any consequences thereof and it is deemed a waiver of any Liability on LIMOVI with regard to such settlement and Jewellery.

12. GOLD LOAN

12.1 Subject to eligibility, the Customer may seek a Gold Loan against eligible Enrolled Jewellery through a lending partner.

12.2 The lending relationship shall be solely between the Customer and the lender.

12.3 Interest, tenure, LTV, repayment, security, default, enforcement, auction and closure shall be governed solely by the lender’s documentation.

12.4 The effect of the loan upon Experience eligibility shall be governed by the applicable Service Terms.

13. GOLD LIQUIDITY

13.1 The Customer may request sale or liquidation of eligible Enrolled Jewellery in accordance with the Gold Liquidity / Sale Agreement.

13.2 Upon completion of a valid sale, title to the sold Jewellery shall pass in accordance with the applicable sale transaction.

13.3 Following completion of such sale, the relevant Jewellery shall cease to be Enrolled Jewellery.

14. WITHDRAWAL OF JEWELLERY

14.1 The Customer may request withdrawal of Enrolled Jewellery by giving the notice specified in Schedule 1 and shall return or surrender the Onboarding Certificate or otherwise complete the prescribed electronic authentication before release, where required..

14.2 Release shall be subject to completion of: (a) pending Experiences; (b) return and physical verification; (c) damage assessment, where applicable; (d) settlement of Owner Benefits and charges; (e) insurance or claims formalities; (f) custody and logistics formalities; and (g) other applicable exit requirements.

14.3 The Customer shall not demand immediate physical release while the Jewellery is subject to an ongoing Experience, transit, claim, inspection, verification, security process or other authorised process.

15. SUSPENSION

15.1 LIMOVI may suspend listing, Experience or other rights relating to Jewellery where: (a) ownership is disputed; (b) title is defective; (c) purity or authenticity is questioned; (d) insurance is unavailable; (e) the condition of the Jewellery creates a material risk; (f) security is compromised; (g) legal compliance is in question; or (h) such suspension is otherwise reasonably necessary under the MCA or Applicable Law.

15.2 Upon receiving credible information concerning the death or legal incapacity of the Owner, LIMOVI may suspend further Experience and release of the Jewellery pending verification of the person legally entitled to receive the Jewellery.

15.3 Release shall be made only upon production and verification of such nomination records, succession documents, probate, letters of administration, court order or other evidence of legal entitlement as may be required.

15.4 The nomination of a person on the LIMOVI Platform shall not, by itself, operate as a transfer of ownership except to the extent recognised under Applicable Law.

16. WARRANTIES AND INDEMNITY

16.1 The Customer warrants the lawful title, authenticity and accuracy of all ownership, purity and other records supplied to LIMOVI.

16.2 The Customer shall indemnify LIMOVI, its affiliates, custodians, service providers and authorised representatives against third-party claims, losses, liabilities and reasonable costs arising from: (a) breach of title warranty; (b) undisclosed encumbrance; (c) third-party ownership claim; (d) forged or inaccurate documentation; or (e) other material misrepresentation concerning the Jewellery.

16.3 The Customer shall immediately notify LIMOVI of any subsequent claim, pledge, charge, dispute, loss of title or other adverse event affecting the Jewellery.

17. RECORDS

LIMOVI may maintain electronic records concerning: (a) Jewellery identification; (b) custody; (c) Experience; (d) Owner Benefits; (e) charges; (f) insurance; (g) withdrawal; and (h) other transactions relating to the Enrolled Jewellery.

18. TERMINATION AND CONSEQUENCES

18.1 Either Party may terminate the Strategy in accordance with the MCA and applicable Strategy Terms.

18.2 Termination shall not affect: (a) ownership of the Customer’s Jewellery; (b) accrued Owner Benefits; (c) completed Experiences; (d) pending liabilities; or (e) any rights or obligations which by their nature survive termination.

18.3 LIMOVI may immediately terminate the enrolment of particular Jewellery where the Jewellery becomes ineligible, is found to be counterfeit or unlawfully sourced, is subject to a material ownership dispute, cannot reasonably be insured or handled, or where continuation of the Service is prohibited or becomes commercially or operationally impracticable. LIMOVI’s decision shall be final with respect to such termination.

18.4 Release of Jewellery following termination shall remain subject to the applicable verification, settlement, Experience, insurance and custody procedures.

19. GOVERNING LAW AND DISPUTE RESOLUTION

This Strategy Agreement shall be governed by the laws of India and shall be subject to the dispute-resolution and jurisdiction provisions of the MCA.
