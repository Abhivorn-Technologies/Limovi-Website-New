2. NATURE AND PURPOSE OF THE INVESTMENT ONLY STRATEGY

2.1 The Investment Only Strategy is an investment-oriented Digital Gold strategy under which the Customer makes an investment through such permitted modes as may be made available by LIMOVI, and the corresponding value is converted into or represented by Gold Balance in accordance with the applicable Digital Gold transaction terms.

2.2 Under this Strategy, the Customer’s investment shall ultimately be represented by a corresponding quantity of Gold Balance credited to the Customer’s Account.

2.3 The Investment Only Strategy does not, merely by reason of the Customer’s participation therein, confer ownership upon the Customer of any specific item of physical Jewellery.

2.4 Any physical Jewellery made available to the Customer under the Jewellery Experience is made available solely as an Experience or Service and shall not, merely by reason of such Experience, become the property of the Customer.

2.5 The Customer’s underlying investment under this Strategy shall remain Digital Gold notwithstanding the Customer’s availing of any Service linked to the Customer’s Gold Balance.

3. ELIGIBILITY

3.1 The Customer may participate in the Investment Only Strategy upon: (a) completion of the prescribed onboarding process; (b) completion of applicable KYC and verification requirements; (c) satisfaction of eligibility criteria; (d) payment of applicable amounts; and (e) acceptance of the MCA, this Strategy Agreement and applicable Service Terms.

3.2 The minimum Gold Balance required for eligibility for any Service shall be the threshold specified in Schedule 1.

3.4 Eligibility for each Service shall be determined by reference to the Customer’s Gold Balance, applicable category limits, availability, Service-specific requirements and such other conditions as may be specified by LIMOVI.

4. GOLD BALANCE AND ACQUISITION OF DIGITAL GOLD

4.1 For the purposes of this Strategy, “Gold Balance” means the quantity of Digital Gold recorded to the credit of the Customer’s Account pursuant to Transactions completed under the Investment Only Strategy. The Gold Balance shall constitute the electronic/account record of the Customer’s Digital Gold holding for the purposes of administering the Strategy and determining the Customer’s eligibility for applicable Services and shall not constitute a separate asset distinct from the Digital Gold to which such balance relates.

4.2 Subject to the applicable eligibility, verification and transaction requirements, the Customer may acquire or augment Digital Gold through such permitted payment, investment or conversion mechanisms as LIMOVI may make available from time to time.

4.3 Where LIMOVI expressly permits the Customer to contribute or convert an existing gold asset, including gold, gold coins, gold bars or Jewellery, such asset shall, upon acceptance and completion of the applicable verification, valuation and transaction formalities, be converted into the corresponding quantity of Digital Gold in accordance with the applicable transaction terms. Upon completion of such conversion, the relevant physical asset shall cease to constitute an asset held by the Customer under the Investment Only Strategy and the corresponding quantity of Digital Gold shall constitute the Customer’s Gold Balance.

4.4 Upon completion of the relevant Transaction, the corresponding quantity of Digital Gold shall be credited to the Customer’s Account. The quantity of Digital Gold so credited shall be determined in accordance with the applicable gold price, purity, weight, valuation date, acquisition or conversion methodology, applicable charges, taxes and other transaction parameters applicable to the relevant Transaction.

4.5 Any increase or decrease in the Gold Balance shall correspond to the applicable acquisition, conversion, sale, transfer, gift, adjustment or other Transaction affecting the Customer’s Digital Gold.

4.6 LIMOVI shall maintain electronic records of the Customer’s Digital Gold holding and all credits, debits, transfers, conversions, sales, adjustments and other Transactions affecting the Gold Balance. Such electronic records shall constitute the primary administrative record of the Customer’s Transactions and Gold Balance, subject to correction of manifest error and the Customer’s rights under Applicable Law.

5. DIGITAL GOLD AND UNDERLYING GOLD BACKING

5.1 Digital Gold acquired under this Strategy shall be backed by corresponding physical gold maintained through MMTC-PAMP or such other bullion partner as LIMOVI may designate, subject to the applicable Digital Gold arrangement.

5.2 The rights and obligations relating to the Customer’s Digital Gold, including the nature and extent of the Customer’s ownership or beneficial interest, the corresponding physical gold backing, custody, transfer, sale, redemption and settlement, shall be governed by the applicable Digital Gold transaction terms, the relevant Transaction Record and Applicable Law.

5.3 LIMOVI shall not acquire ownership or title to the Customer’s Digital Gold merely by reason of receiving payment, recording, administering, facilitating or maintaining the Customer’s Digital Gold holding, except to the extent expressly provided under the applicable transaction structure or Applicable Law.

5.4 The Customer’s rights and entitlements in respect of Digital Gold shall at all times be subject to the applicable Digital Gold transaction terms, Transaction Records and Applicable Law.

5.5 The physical gold maintained by the applicable bullion partner shall constitute the backing for the Digital Gold in accordance with the applicable Digital Gold arrangement and shall be maintained, held and administered in accordance with the terms applicable to such arrangement.

5.6 The physical gold maintained as backing for the Customer’s Digital Gold shall not, merely by reason of such backing, constitute Jewellery owned by the Customer, form part of the Jewellery Cloud or become available to the Customer as physical Jewellery under the Jewellery Experience.

5.7 The minimum purchase amount of Digital Gold and the minimum Gold Balance required for eligibility for any Service shall be as specified in Schedule 1 or the applicable Service Terms.
