These Gold Liquidity and Sale Terms (“Agreement”) constitute the specific terms applicable to the Gold Liquidity Service made available by LIMOVI Gold Ecosystem Private Limited (“LIMOVI”) under the LIMOVI Master Customer & Platform Agreement (“Master Agreement”).

These Terms shall apply to every Gold Liquidity Transaction initiated or confirmed by a Customer through the LIMOVI Platform and shall be read together with the Master Agreement and the applicable Strategy Terms.

The Customer's use of the Gold Liquidity Service, initiation of a Gold Liquidity Transaction or electronic acceptance of these Terms shall constitute the Customer's acceptance of this Agreement.

This Agreement governs each Gold Liquidity Transaction pursuant to which eligible Gold, Gold Balance and/or Jewellery is sold or otherwise liquidated by the Customer in accordance with the applicable Service.

1. PURPOSE AND SCOPE

1.1 Subject to eligibility, verification and Applicable Law, the Customer may request a Gold Liquidity Transaction through the LIMOVI Platform.

1.2 These Terms govern each Gold Liquidity Transaction undertaken by a Customer through the LIMOVI Platform.

1.3 The Customer's eligibility to avail the Gold Liquidity Service shall be determined in accordance with the Master Agreement and the applicable Strategy Terms.

1.4 The nature of the asset eligible for liquidation shall depend upon the Customer's applicable Strategy and the specific transaction confirmation.

1.5 The applicable Transaction Confirmation shall specify the quantity, weight, purity, valuation, deductions and net consideration, as applicable.

2. ELIGIBLE GOLD OR JEWELLERY

2.1 Gold Liquidity may, as applicable to the Customer's Strategy, relate to:

(a) Gold Balance or eligible Gold corresponding to the Customer's holding;

(b) Digital Gold, where expressly enabled and subject to the applicable Digital Gold arrangement;

(c) Jewellery owned by the Customer; or

(d) Enrolled Jewellery eligible for sale under the applicable Strategy.

2.2 No Gold or Jewellery shall be eligible for liquidation where the Customer's right, title or entitlement is subject to an unresolved dispute, pledge, lien, charge, legal restriction, compliance hold or other restriction.

2.3 Where the Gold is subject to a Gold Loan, liquidation shall be subject to closure or release of the relevant security arrangement.

3. CUSTOMER'S TITLE AND AUTHORITY

3.1 The Customer represents that the Customer has lawful title to, or lawful authority to liquidate, the Gold or Jewellery submitted for the Gold Liquidity Transaction.

3.2 The Customer shall not submit Gold or Jewellery which is stolen, unlawfully sourced, pledged without disclosure, encumbered or subject to any third-party claim.

3.3 LIMOVI may require proof of ownership, acquisition, custody, purity or other entitlement before accepting the transaction.

4. LIQUIDITY REQUEST

4.1 The Customer may initiate a Gold Liquidity request through the Platform or such other mechanism authorised by LIMOVI.

4.2 The Customer shall specify the quantity, Jewellery item or portion of Gold proposed to be liquidated.

4.3 A request shall not constitute a completed sale until the transaction has been verified and confirmed in accordance with this Agreement.

5. VERIFICATION

5.1 LIMOVI may verify:

(a) Customer KYC;

(b) Account status;

(c) Gold Balance;

(d) ownership or entitlement;

(e) weight and purity;

(f) Jewellery identification;

(g) applicable loan, lien or charge status; and

(h) such other information as may reasonably be required.

5.2 LIMOVI may defer, reject or cancel a transaction where the required verification is not satisfactorily completed.

5.3 For physical Jewellery, LIMOVI may conduct weighing, purity testing, inspection, hallmark verification, photography and condition assessment before determining the final sale value.

5.4 The Customer understands and acknowledges that LIMOVI may necessarily require to assay the Jewellery by melting or such other procedures to determine the purity, weight and subsequent valuation. Any such Destructive or Semi-destructive methodologies used for the said purposes are irreversible. LIMOVI shall notify the Customer of any requirement of such methodologies in writing at the time of Customer's request for Liquidation. LIMOVI shall not be responsible for loss of the Jewellery caused due to such methodologies conducted subsequent to the confirmation by the Customer of such liquidation. All the returns against such Liquidation shall be subject to the purity, weight and valuation confirmed after the completion of such methodologies.

6. VALUATION AND MARKET PRICE

6.1 The consideration payable for Gold Liquidity shall be determined by applying the applicable gold price or valuation methodology prevailing at the relevant time.

6.2 In respect of Jewellery liquidated under the Enrol & Experience Strategy and Investment & Experience Strategy, the applicable sale value shall be determined with reference to the prevailing market price of 22K gold per gram, applied to the net gold weight accepted by LIMOVI upon verification, subject to the applicable Transaction Confirmation and deductions prescribed under this Agreement.

6.3 The applicable price per gram, weight, total value and other liquidation particulars may be displayed on the Customer's Dashboard or communicated in the Transaction Confirmation.

6.4 For other Strategies or asset types, the applicable purity and valuation basis shall be the one specified in the relevant Strategy Terms, Commercial Schedule or Transaction Confirmation.

6.5 The Customer acknowledges that gold prices may fluctuate and that an indicative or previously displayed price shall not remain binding until the Transaction is duly confirmed.

7. DETERMINATION OF WEIGHT AND PURITY

7.1 For physical Gold or Jewellery, the final payable amount shall be based on the weight and purity accepted by LIMOVI following verification.

7.2 Where Jewellery contains stones, non-gold components or other materials, the applicable valuation methodology shall determine whether and to what extent such components are included in the sale value.

7.3 If the actual purity or weight materially differs from the particulars declared by the Customer, LIMOVI may revise the valuation or reject the transaction.

8. SALE PRICE AND NET CONSIDERATION

8.1 The gross sale consideration shall be calculated in accordance with the applicable valuation methodology.

8.2 The following may be deducted, to the extent applicable:

(a) TDS or other withholding;

(b) GST or other applicable taxes;

(c) statutory levies;

(d) payment or bank transfer charges;

(e) platform or service charges expressly applicable to the transaction; and

(f) other deductions expressly disclosed to the Customer or required by Applicable Law.

8.3 The Net Sale Consideration shall be the amount remaining after giving effect to the applicable deductions.

8.4 The final calculation shall be reflected in the Liquidity Transaction Statement or Transaction Confirmation.

9. CONFIRMATION OF SALE

9.1 The Gold Liquidity Transaction shall become binding upon the confirmation event specified in the Platform, Transaction Confirmation or applicable Service Terms.

9.2 Upon such confirmation, the Customer authorises LIMOVI to complete the sale, apply the applicable valuation, give effect to the relevant deductions and settle the Net Sale Consideration.

9.3 Except where cancellation or reversal is expressly permitted under Applicable Law or the Transaction Confirmation, the Customer shall not be entitled to revoke a completed or confirmed sale.

10. TRANSFER OF TITLE

10.1 Upon completion of the Gold Liquidity Transaction and settlement of the applicable consideration, ownership and rights of the Customer in respect of the liquidated Gold or Jewellery shall cease to the extent of the quantity sold.

10.2 In the case of Jewellery, the Customer shall cease to have any ownership or proprietary claim over the Jewellery once the sale has been completed.

10.3 The transfer of title shall not affect the Customer's obligations relating to any prior transaction, undisclosed encumbrance, fraud, incorrect declaration or other liability arising under the contractual documents.

11. SETTLEMENT

11.1 The Net Sale Consideration shall be credited to the Customer's registered and verified bank account.

11.2 Subject to successful completion of all verification, processing and banking requirements, LIMOVI shall endeavour to credit the Net Sale Consideration to the Customer's registered and verified bank account within one hour from confirmation of the Gold Liquidity Transaction, after giving effect to all applicable TDS, GST, statutory deductions, banking charges and other permitted deductions.

11.3 LIMOVI shall not be responsible for delay solely attributable to a banking institution, payment system, technical intermediary or other third party beyond LIMOVI's reasonable control, except to the extent expressly assumed by LIMOVI or required under Applicable Law.

11.4 The Customer shall ensure that the registered bank account particulars are accurate and current.

12. PARTIAL LIQUIDATION

12.1 Where the Customer liquidates only part of an eligible Gold Balance, the unliquidated balance shall continue to remain subject to the applicable Strategy and Service Terms.

12.2 Where the Customer liquidates the entire eligible balance or all Jewellery constituting the relevant Service position, the consequences specified in the applicable Strategy Terms shall apply.

13. CONSEQUENCES OF LIQUIDATION

13.1 In the Enrol & Experience Strategy and Investment & Experience Strategy, completion of liquidation of the Purchased and Enrolled Jewellery shall result in cessation of the Customer's rights in and to such Jewellery and shall have such further effect on the Customer's Strategy, Services and exit status as is expressly provided under the applicable Strategy Terms.

13.2 Where the applicable Strategy provides that full liquidation results in closure or exit from the Platform, such consequence shall apply upon completion of the Transaction.

13.3 Any Strategy-specific charge, fee, lifetime Experience charge, value-addition contribution or other amount already applied shall not be refundable merely because the Customer subsequently elects to liquidate, except where expressly provided otherwise in the applicable Strategy Terms.

(a) KYC or ownership verification is incomplete;

(d) purity or weight cannot be satisfactorily determined;

(e) the transaction raises fraud, security, compliance or legal concerns; or

(f) processing is prohibited or restricted under Applicable Law.
