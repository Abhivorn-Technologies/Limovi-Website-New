1. PURPOSE

1.1 LIMOVI Gold Ecosystem Private Limited (“LIMOVI”, “Company, “we”, “us” or “our”) is committed to providing a transparent and accessible mechanism for receiving, registering, examining and resolving complaints, grievances and service-related concerns raised by persons using the LIMOVI Platform or Services.

1.2 This Grievance Redressal Policy (“Policy”) establishes the mechanism through which a Customer, Registered User, Visitor or other person may communicate a grievance to LIMOVI and provides for its examination, escalation and resolution.

1.3 This Policy is intended to supplement the contractual rights and remedies available under the LIMOVI Master Customer & Platform Agreement (“Master Agreement”), applicable Service Terms and applicable law.

2. SCOPE

2.1 This Policy applies to grievances relating to the LIMOVI website, mobile application, customer account, onboarding, KYC processes, Gold Balance, Jewellery-related Services, Experience Services, payments, charges, delivery or return of Jewellery, communications, third-party service arrangements administered through LIMOVI and other Services made available by LIMOVI.

2.2 Where a complaint concerns an activity performed directly by a third-party regulated entity or service provider, LIMOVI may coordinate with the relevant entity or direct the complainant to the appropriate grievance mechanism of that entity, without prejudice to any obligation applicable to LIMOVI.

3. WHAT CONSTITUTES A GRIEVANCE

3.1 For the purposes of this Policy, a “Grievance” means a complaint, concern or dissatisfaction relating to a LIMOVI Platform or Service which requires examination or response by LIMOVI.

A grievance may include, among other matters, concerns regarding service delivery, account-related matters, charges, transaction processing, Jewellery handling, customer support, communication, privacy, KYC processes, delays, technical issues or conduct of persons acting on behalf of LIMOVI.

3.2 A request for general information or assistance which does not involve a complaint may be dealt with through LIMOVI's ordinary customer-support mechanism.

4. GRIEVANCE REDRESSAL OFFICER

4.1 LIMOVI shall designate a person as its Grievance Redressal Officer (“Grievance Officer”) for receiving and coordinating the resolution of grievances under this Policy.

4.2 The details of the Grievance Officer shall be published on the LIMOVI Platform and may be updated by LIMOVI from time to time.

4.3 The Grievance Officer may be assisted by such employees, officers or service providers as LIMOVI considers appropriate for examination and resolution of grievances.

5. MODE OF SUBMISSION

5.1 A grievance may be submitted through the customer-support facility, email address, application interface, written communication or such other channel as LIMOVI may make available.

5.2 The complainant should provide sufficient information to enable LIMOVI to identify the relevant person, account, transaction or Service and understand the nature of the grievance.

5.3 Where relevant, the complainant should provide the transaction reference, Customer identification details, date of the relevant event and supporting documents or other evidence available to the complainant.

5.4 LIMOVI may request additional information where the information initially provided is insufficient to examine the grievance.

6. ACKNOWLEDGEMENT

6.1 LIMOVI shall endeavour to acknowledge receipt of a grievance within four to six (4-6) business days of receiving the complaint through an authorised grievance channel.

6.2 The acknowledgement may contain a reference or ticket number through which the grievance may be identified and subsequently tracked.

6.3 Where the communication does not contain sufficient information to identify or examine the grievance, LIMOVI may request the complainant to provide the necessary particulars.

7. EXAMINATION OF GRIEVANCE

7.1 Upon receipt of a grievance, LIMOVI shall examine the matter having regard to the information provided by the complainant, relevant account and transaction records, applicable contractual documents, internal records and information obtained from relevant employees, partners or service providers.

7.2 Where a grievance concerns a third party, LIMOVI may obtain information from or refer the matter to the relevant third party for examination.

7.3 LIMOVI may seek clarification from the complainant where necessary for determining the facts or appropriate resolution of the matter.

8. RESOLUTION AND RESPONSE

8.1 LIMOVI shall endeavour to resolve and communicate its decision on a grievance within Fifteen (15) days from receipt of the grievance, subject to the nature and complexity of the matter.

8.2 Where a grievance cannot reasonably be resolved within the applicable period because it requires investigation, information from a third party, examination of physical Jewellery or other circumstances beyond the immediate control of LIMOVI, the complainant shall be informed of the status of the grievance and where reasonably practicable, the expected further course of action.

8.3 The response shall, to the extent appropriate, communicate the outcome of the examination and any action proposed or taken by LIMOVI.

8.4 LIMOVI shall not be liable for any delay in resolution or non-resolution of any grievance in the event such delay or non-resolution arises from (a) the complainant’s failure to provide information or documentation reasonably required by LIMOVI for examination of the grievance or (b) the complainant's failure to complete a verification, documentation or procedural step that is a necessary precondition for LIMOVI to examine or act upon the grievance (such as, without limitation, providing access to Jewellery for physical inspection, or completing an identity-verification step required to process a request, provide an in person clarification ). For clarity, a complainant's decision to decline a proposed resolution, seek review, or escalate the grievance shall not, by itself, be treated as non-cooperation under this Clause.

9. ESCALATION

9.1 Where the complainant is not satisfied with the response or the grievance remains unresolved, the complainant may request escalation to the designated senior officer or such other authority within LIMOVI as may be specified from time to time.

9.2 An escalation request should ordinarily refer to the original grievance reference number and state the grounds on which the complainant seeks further review.

9.3 The designated officer may review the original grievance, the response provided and any additional material submitted by the complainant before communicating the further decision of LIMOVI.

10. SERVICE-PARTNER GRIEVANCES

10.1 Certain LIMOVI Services may involve third-party entities, including regulated entities, NBFCs, payment service providers, Digital Gold providers, vaulting or security providers, Jewellery manufacturers, insurance providers or other service providers.

10.2 Where the subject matter of a grievance falls within the independent responsibility of such third party, LIMOVI may coordinate with the relevant entity or communicate the appropriate grievance mechanism to the complainant.

10.3 Where applicable law or the contractual arrangement requires the grievance to be submitted directly to the relevant regulated entity, the complainant may be required to pursue the grievance through that entity's prescribed mechanism.

10.4 Nothing in this Clause shall prevent LIMOVI from assisting the complainant or coordinating with the relevant service provider where appropriate.

11. PRIVACY AND CONFIDENTIALITY

11.1 Information provided in connection with a grievance shall be handled in accordance with LIMOVI's Privacy Policy and applicable law.

11.2 LIMOVI shall restrict access to grievance-related information to persons having a legitimate requirement to examine, investigate or resolve the grievance, subject to applicable legal and regulatory requirements.

11.3 A complainant shall not be required to disclose information unrelated to the grievance merely for the purpose of submitting a complaint.

12. FRAUDULENT OR ABUSIVE COMPLAINTS

12.1 LIMOVI shall provide access to the grievance mechanism in good faith and without discrimination.

12.2 Where a complaint is knowingly false, materially misleading, fraudulent, abusive or submitted for an unlawful purpose, LIMOVI may take appropriate action in accordance with the Master Agreement and applicable law.

12.3 Nothing in this Clause shall prevent a person from raising a genuine grievance merely because the grievance is ultimately found to be without merit.

13. NO RETALIATION

13.1 LIMOVI shall not take retaliatory action merely because a person has submitted a genuine grievance or sought review of a decision through the grievance mechanism.

13.2 This Clause shall not restrict LIMOVI from taking lawful action in relation to fraud, abuse, unlawful conduct, breach of contractual obligations or misuse of the Platform.

14. RECORDS AND MONITORING

14.1 LIMOVI shall maintain appropriate records of grievances received, their status, correspondence, investigation and resolution in accordance with its internal record-retention requirements and applicable law. LIMOVI reserves the right to record any calls, communications with respect to the grievances for record and legal purposes to the extent permitted by law provided that where a call is recorded, the complainant shall be informed of such recording at the commencement of the call.

14.2 LIMOVI may periodically review grievance records to identify recurring complaints, operational deficiencies, service-provider issues, customer-impacting problems or areas requiring improvement and may retain such records for such periods as may be required.

14.3 The Company may use aggregated or appropriately anonymised grievance information for internal quality control, risk management, compliance and service improvement.

15. CUSTOMER SUPPORT AND GRIEVANCE DISTINGUISHED

15.1 The ordinary customer-support mechanism may be used for routine assistance, information requests and operational queries.

15.2 Where a customer-support matter remains unresolved or constitutes a formal complaint, it may be escalated and treated as a grievance under this Policy.

15.3 The availability of customer support shall not prevent a person from directly approaching the Grievance Officer in accordance with this Policy.

16. DEEMED RESOLUTION

16.1 A grievance shall be deemed resolved upon the occurrence of any of the following, whichever is earlier:

(a) the complainant confirms, in writing or through the Platform's grievance-tracking mechanism, acceptance of the resolution communicated by LIMOVI;

(b) LIMOVI communicates its decision and proposed resolution, and the complainant does not request escalation under Clause 9 within ________ ( ) days of such communication, in which case the resolution shall be deemed accepted by the complainant;

(c) where the grievance has been escalated under Clause 9, upon communication of LIMOVI's final decision following such escalation; or

(d) where the grievance has been referred to or independently pursued before an external forum described in Clause 10 or otherwise available under Applicable Law, upon final disposal by such forum.

8A.2 A grievance deemed resolved under this Clause shall be closed and recorded accordingly for the purposes of LIMOVI's internal records. LIMOVI may reopen a closed grievance at its discretion where new information material to the grievance is found.

8A.3 Nothing in this Clause shall be construed as extinguishing, waiving or limiting any statutory right of the complainant to pursue the grievance before any competent authority or court, regardless of whether the grievance has been deemed resolved under this Clause for LIMOVI's internal purposes.

17. This Policy shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of India. The Master Agreement, applicable Service Terms and other contractual documents shall continue to govern the substantive rights and obligations of LIMOVI and its Customers. Nothing in this Policy shall be interpreted as replacing the dispute-resolution, arbitration, jurisdiction or other contractual provisions contained in the applicable agreement.

18. AMENDMENT OF POLICY

18.1 LIMOVI may amend this Policy from time to time to reflect changes in its business operations, Services, applicable law, regulatory requirements or grievance-management procedures.

18.2 The revised Policy shall be made available through the LIMOVI Platform or such other channel as LIMOVI may consider appropriate.
