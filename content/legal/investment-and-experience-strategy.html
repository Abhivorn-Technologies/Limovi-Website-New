This Investment & Experience Strategy Agreement (“Strategy Agreement”) is entered into pursuant to and forms an integral part of the LIMOVI Master Customer & Platform Agreement (“MCA”) and governs the Customer’s participation in the Investment & Experience Strategy.

PARTIES:

LIMOVI GOLD ECOSYSTEM PRIVATE LIMITED, a company incorporated under the Companies Act, 2013, having its registered office at [____________] (“LIMOVI”);

AND

The customer identified in the applicable onboarding record, Account, Transaction Record or other electronic record (“Customer”).

LIMOVI and the Customer are collectively referred to as the “Parties”.

1. STATUS AND INCORPORATION UNDER THE MCA

1.1This Strategy Agreement is a Strategy-specific agreement entered into under the MCA and constitutes an integral part of the contractual relationship between LIMOVI and the Customer.

1.2 The Customer’s participation in this Strategy shall additionally be governed by: (a) the MCA; (b) this Strategy Agreement; (c) Schedule 1; (d) the applicable Transaction Record; (e) the Common Jewellery Experience Terms; (f) the applicable Jewellery procurement/ manufacturing arrangements; (g) the applicable Gold Liquidity / Sale Agreement; (h) applicable lending partner documentation; and (i) applicable Insurance Documentation.

1.3 Capitalised terms not defined herein shall have the meanings assigned to them in the MCA.

1.4 The MCA shall govern all general matters. This Strategy Agreement shall govern matters specifically relating to the Investment & Experience Strategy.

1.5 In the event of inconsistency:

(a) transaction-specific documents shall prevail in respect of transaction-specific particulars;

(b) Schedule 1 shall prevail in respect of commercial particulars expressly specified therein;

(c) this Strategy Agreement shall prevail in respect of Strategy-specific matters; and

(d) the MCA shall prevail in respect of general matters.

2. NATURE AND PURPOSE OF THE STRATEGY

2.1 Under the Investment & Experience Strategy, the Customer makes the Investment Amount specified in the applicable Transaction Record and becomes entitled, subject to this Strategy Agreement, to select qualifying Jewellery and acquire ownership of such Jewellery upon completion of the applicable acquisition or manufacturing formalities.

2.2 The Customer shall additionally receive Experience entitlements in accordance with this Strategy Agreement and the applicable Service Terms.

2.3 The Investment Amount, Strategy Charge, Jewellery allocation, Experience entitlement and other commercial components shall be determined in accordance with Schedule 1 and the applicable Transaction Record.

3. MINIMUM INVESTMENT AND ACTIVATION

3.1 The minimum Investment Amount shall be the amount equivalent to 50 grams of 24K gold or such other amount as specified in Schedule 1.

3.2 Activation of the Strategy shall be conditional upon: (a) completion of KYC; (b) verification; (c) receipt of the Investment Amount; (d) acceptance of the MCA and this Strategy Agreement; and (e) satisfaction of other applicable conditions precedent.

3.3 The Investment Amount shall be recorded in the Customer’s Account and shall constitute the basis for determining the Customer’s entitlements under the Strategy.

4. ALLOCATION OF INVESTMENT AMOUNT

4.1 The Investment Amount shall be allocated between: (a) the Strategy Charge; and (b) the Jewellery component, in accordance with Schedule 1.

4.2 The Strategy Charge shall confer upon the Customer the Experience entitlements expressly specified in this Strategy Agreement and Schedule 1.

4.3 The Jewellery allocation shall represent the portion of the Investment Amount attributable to the acquisition or manufacture of the Jewellery, subject to the applicable commercial terms.

4.4 The allocation shall be recorded in the applicable Transaction Record and, once confirmed, shall not be altered except as expressly permitted under the applicable Transaction or exit terms.

5. JEWELLERY SELECTION

5.1 The Customer may select qualifying Jewellery through the Jewellery Cloud or such catalogue as LIMOVI may make available.

5.2 Selection shall be subject to: (a) category; (b) availability; (c) applicable value limits;(d) design specifications; (e) location; (f) manufacturing feasibility; and (g) other applicable conditions.

5.3 No Jewellery selection shall become binding upon LIMOVI until expressly confirmed.

5.4 LIMOVI may procure or arrange manufacture of selected Jewellery through its appointed Jewellery Manufacturer or other authorised supplier.

6. ACQUISITION AND OWNERSHIP OF JEWELLERY

6.1 The Customer shall become the owner of the Jewellery acquired or manufactured under this Strategy upon completion of the applicable acquisition and transaction formalities specified in the Transaction Record.

6.2 Prior to such completion, the Customer’s rights shall be limited to the contractual entitlement created under this Strategy Agreement and shall not be construed as ownership of an unidentified or unacquired Jewellery item.

6.3 Upon acquisition, the Jewellery shall be identified through a Jewellery Record, Certificate or equivalent record containing, where applicable: (a) weight; (b) purity; (c) design; (d) description; (e) identification number; and (f) other relevant particulars.

6.4 LIMOVI may retain possession of Customer-owned Jewellery for custody, security and Experience enablement without acquiring title thereto.

6.5 The Customer’s ownership shall remain distinct from any temporary possession or usage rights granted to an Experience Customer.

7. JEWELLERY VALUE

7.1 The value of Jewellery available for selection shall be the percentage of the Investment Amount specified in Schedule 1.

7.2 Unless otherwise specified, the applicable valuation may comprise: (a) 22K gold content; (b) stone cost; (c) making or value-addition component; and (d) applicable GST, in accordance with the applicable Transaction Record.

7.3 The Customer acknowledges that the Investment Amount and Jewellery value are contractual commercial components of the Strategy and shall be determined in accordance with Schedule 1 and the applicable Transaction Record.

8. EXPERIENCE ENTITLEMENT

8.1 The Customer shall be entitled to the number and categories of Experience transactions specified in Schedule 1.

8.2 Where Schedule 1 provides for free Experiences, such entitlement shall remain subject to: (a) the applicable Strategy period; (b) Service Charges; (c) category restrictions; (d) availability; and (e) the Common Jewellery Experience Terms.

8.3 Additional Experiences shall be chargeable at the applicable rate after exhaustion of the Customer’s free entitlement.

8.4 The detailed operational terms governing delivery, temporary possession, use, return, damage, loss and insurance shall be governed by the Common Jewellery Experience Terms.

9. EXPERIENCE AND SERVICE CHARGES

9.1 The Customer shall pay the applicable Service Charge for each Experience.

9.2 The Customer shall pay the applicable Experience Charge for any Experience exceeding the free entitlement.

9.3 Location-specific Service Charges and Lifestyle Jewellery subscription charges shall be as specified in Schedule 1 or confirmed at the time of the relevant request.

9.4 Taxes and statutory levies shall be payable in addition to stated charges unless expressly stated to be inclusive.

10. LIFESTYLE JEWELLERY

10.1 Subject to eligibility, the Customer may avail Lifestyle Jewellery up to the limit specified in Schedule 1.

10.2 Subscription-based Lifestyle Jewellery shall be governed by the applicable subscription period, charges and Service Terms.

10.3 Lifestyle Jewellery shall not alter the Customer’s ownership rights in the Jewellery acquired under this Strategy unless expressly provided under a separate transaction.

11. GOLD LOAN

11.1 Subject to eligibility, the Customer may seek a Gold Loan against eligible Customer-owned Jewellery through an eligible lending partner.

11.2 The lender alone shall determine: (a) interest; (b) tenure; (c) LTV; (d) repayment; (e) security; (f) default; (g) enforcement; (h) auction; and (i) closure.

11.3 Where the maximum permissible loan is availed, Experience eligibility may be suspended to the extent specified in Schedule 1 and applicable Service Terms.

12. GOLD LIQUIDITY

12.1 The Customer may request liquidation of eligible Gold or Customer-owned Jewellery in accordance with the Gold Liquidity / Sale Agreement.

12.2 The applicable sale price, deductions, taxes, settlement and effect of liquidation upon Strategy benefits shall be determined by the applicable transaction documentation.

12.3 No refund of the Strategy Charge shall arise solely by reason of subsequent liquidation unless expressly provided in Schedule 1 or applicable exit terms.

13. GIFT OR TRANSFER OF GOLD BALANCE

13.1 Subject to eligibility, the Customer may gift or transfer eligible Gold Balance through the mechanism prescribed by LIMOVI.

13.2 The recipient shall satisfy all applicable onboarding, KYC and acceptance requirements.

13.3 Upon completion of the transfer, the recipient shall be bound by the applicable terms governing the transferred Gold Balance.

14. JEWELLERY AS AN ASSET AND OWNER BENEFIT

14.1 Where Customer-owned Jewellery acquired under this Strategy is made available through the Jewellery Cloud and selected by another eligible Customer for Experience, the Customer shall be entitled to the Owner Benefit specified in Schedule 1.

14.2 The Owner Benefit shall arise only upon completion of a qualifying Experience.

14.3 The Owner Benefit shall be subject to: (a) applicable charges; (b) taxes; (c) deductions; (d) reconciliation; and (e) settlement procedures.

14.4 LIMOVI shall maintain records of qualifying Experiences and corresponding Owner Benefits.

15. CUSTODY AND INSURANCE

15.1 Following acquisition, LIMOVI may retain or arrange custody of Customer-owned Jewellery with a Vault Provider, Custodian, security partner or other authorised service provider.

15.2 The Jewellery may be moved between authorised locations for custody, Experience, inspection, insurance, verification or logistics.

15.3 Insurance shall be governed by the applicable insurance arrangement.

15.4 The insurer shall determine the terms of the applicable policy, including insured value, coverage, exclusions, deductibles and claims.

15.5 The Customer shall not maintain outside insurance in respect of the same Jewellery where the applicable LIMOVI arrangement requires the Jewellery to remain under the designated insurance arrangement.

16. EXIT

16.1 The Customer may request exit after the minimum participation period specified in Schedule 1.

16.2 Exit shall be subject to: (a) prescribed notice; (b) closure of outstanding Services; (c) settlement of pending Transactions; (d) settlement of applicable charges; (e) completion or closure of pending Experiences; (f) applicable custody and verification requirements; and (g) other exit formalities.

16.3 The available exit option shall be the option expressly specified in Schedule 1 and may include: (a) delivery or retention of Customer-owned Jewellery; or (b) settlement in Gold, where expressly permitted.

16.4 The applicable valuation date, purity, value-addition component, taxes and other adjustments shall be specified in the Exit Statement.

17. SUSPENSION AND TERMINATION

17.1 LIMOVI may suspend Experience rights or terminate the Strategy in accordance with the MCA upon: (a) fraud; (b) material breach; (c) non-payment; (d) repeated misuse; (e) compliance concerns; or (f) continuation becoming unlawful or materially impracticable.

17.2 Termination of Experience rights shall not, by itself, extinguish the Customer’s ownership of Customer-owned Jewellery, subject to any lawful lien, security interest, set-off or recovery rights.

17.3 Termination shall not affect accrued Owner Benefits, payment obligations or liabilities relating to completed or pending Transactions.

18. CUSTOMER REPRESENTATIONS AND UNDERTAKINGS

The Customer represents and undertakes that: (a) the Customer has legal capacity to enter into this Strategy Agreement; (b) all information furnished is true and complete; (c) the Investment Amount is paid through permitted means; (d) the Customer shall comply with KYC and Applicable Law; (e) the Customer shall comply with the MCA and applicable Service Terms; and (f) the Customer shall not misuse the Strategy or any Jewellery made available through it.

19. RECORDS AND STATEMENTS

19.1 LIMOVI may maintain electronic records concerning: (a) Investment Amount; (b) Gold Balance; (c) Jewellery; (d) Experience; (e) Owner Benefits; (f) charges; (g) loans; (h) liquidity transactions; and (i) exit settlements.

19.2 Such records may be relied upon for administration, reconciliation and settlement, subject to correction of manifest error and Applicable Law.

20. NO IMPLIED OWNERSHIP OR REFUND

20.1 No ownership in Jewellery shall arise before completion of the acquisition formalities specified in this Strategy Agreement.

20.2 No refund, rebate or reversal of the Strategy Charge shall arise merely because the Customer subsequently elects not to use an Experience entitlement or subsequently liquidates the Jewellery, except where expressly provided under the applicable terms.

21. GOVERNING LAW AND DISPUTE RESOLUTION

This Strategy Agreement shall be governed by the laws of India and shall be subject to the dispute-resolution and jurisdiction provisions of the MCA.
